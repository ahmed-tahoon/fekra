  • Gate 2 — QA: all required items are re-tested and marked QA Passed.
  • Gate 3 — Go-Live: no critical/blocker defect is open; major defects are fixed or explicitly approved by FEKRA in writing.
  • Gate 4 — Handover: FEKRA receives source, access, documentation, final evidence and ownership of agreed project services.
